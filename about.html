Surgical Instruments & Innovations (SII Medical) is the exclusive US distributor of Susol single-use sterile surgical instruments manufactured by Bailey Instruments of England. We serve healthcare providers across America with precision-engineered instruments that eliminate cross-contamination risk while reducing operational costs.

Our goal is to build on our reputation as one of the industry's service leaders, by providing high-quality surgical products to the world's leading healthcare providers in their pursuit of patient-care excellence.

Our Manufacturer

Bailey Instruments

England, est. 1984

With over 35 years of surgical instrument manufacturing expertise, Bailey Instruments produces the Susol range of single-use sterile instruments to the highest standards of precision and quality. Every instrument is engineered with sharper edges, better ergonomics, and consistent performance across every unit produced.

Bailey's commitment to quality means every instrument meets rigorous international standards before it leaves the factory floor. This partnership is the foundation of our promise to healthcare providers: reliable instruments, every single time.

Sterilization Partner

STERIS

Global leader in sterilization services

Every Susol instrument is sterilized by STERIS, a $19+ billion global company and the gold standard in healthcare sterilization. Using ethylene oxide (EtO) sterilization, each instrument arrives factory-sealed and ready for immediate use.

This partnership ensures that every instrument in our catalog meets the most stringent sterilization standards in the industry, giving healthcare providers complete confidence from package to patient.
