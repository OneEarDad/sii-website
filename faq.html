Products & Quality

What are Susol single-use instruments?

Susol instruments are precision-engineered, sterile, single-use surgical instruments manufactured by Bailey Instruments of England. Each instrument is factory-sealed and sterilized by STERIS using ethylene oxide (EtO) sterilization, ready for immediate use straight from the package.

How do single-use instruments compare to reusable ones in quality?

Susol instruments are manufactured to the same precision standards as premium reusable instruments. The key difference is that every Susol instrument is factory-new — you get sharp edges, perfect alignment, and consistent performance every single time. Reusable instruments degrade over repeated sterilization cycles, losing sharpness and precision.

What medical specialties do your instruments serve?

Our catalog serves 9 medical specialties: General Surgery, Podiatry, ENT, Orthopaedics, Gynaecology, Maternity, Wound Care, Vascular, and Diabetes Diagnostics. We offer 31 instruments across 12 categories including forceps, scissors, nippers, curettes, procedure packs, and more.

Are your instruments FDA registered?

Yes. SII Medical is FDA registered under registration number #1413711. All instruments meet rigorous quality and safety standards required for surgical use in US healthcare settings.

Single-Use Benefits

How do single-use instruments reduce infection risk?

Single-use instruments completely eliminate the risk of cross-contamination between patients. Each instrument is factory-sealed and sterile — used once and disposed. There is no reprocessing chain where contamination can occur, breaking the cycle of healthcare associated infections (HAIs).

Are single-use instruments more cost-effective than reusable?

When you factor in the full cost of reprocessing — autoclaves, sterilization staff, chemical supplies, instrument repair, tracking systems, and liability exposure — single-use instruments are often the more cost-effective choice. They eliminate all hidden reprocessing costs while simplifying workflows.

How are the instruments sterilized?

Every Susol instrument is sterilized by STERIS, a $19+ billion global leader in healthcare sterilization, using ethylene oxide (EtO) sterilization — the industry gold standard. Each instrument arrives factory-sealed and ready for immediate use.
